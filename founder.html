Samira Abdirahman Maalim

Founder & Visionary Leader

Goreale Agricultural Innovators was founded by Samira Abdirahman Maalim with a vision of developing practical solutions that connect agriculture, water management, environmental conservation and community development.

The Founder believes that sustainable agricultural development should go beyond increasing food production. It should also promote responsible use of water and land, protect natural resources, restore vegetation and create meaningful opportunities for young people and farming communities.

Through Goreale Agricultural Innovators, the vision is to encourage farmers and communities to adopt climate-smart farming practices, improve water-use efficiency, strengthen crop management, plant trees and participate actively in environmental conservation.

The initiative is built around practical community action, innovation, knowledge sharing, evidence-based learning and partnerships that can contribute to resilient and sustainable agricultural communities.

The Founder’s Vision

To contribute to a future where farmers and communities can produce food sustainably, use water responsibly, restore nature and build resilient livelihoods while responding to environmental and climate challenges.
