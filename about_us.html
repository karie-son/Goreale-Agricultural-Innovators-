About Us

Discover who we are, what drives us and our commitment to sustainable agriculture and environmental conservation.

Who We Are

Goreale Agricultural Innovators is a youth-led agricultural and environmental initiative founded by Samira Abdirahman Maalim to promote climate-smart agriculture, efficient water use, sustainable farming and environmental conservation in Goreale and surrounding communities in Kenya.

We believe that communities with agricultural land can create stronger and more sustainable livelihoods when farmers have access to practical knowledge, appropriate technology and opportunities to test innovative farming solutions.

Our work focuses on connecting agriculture, technology, water efficiency, environmental conservation and youth participation to address challenges affecting farming communities.

Goreale Agricultural Innovators seeks to create a practical platform where farmers, young people, students, agricultural professionals, community organizations, researchers, development partners and other stakeholders can exchange knowledge, develop ideas and work together on solutions that respond to local agricultural and environmental needs.

Our Purpose

Agriculture remains an important source of food, employment and livelihood for many communities. However, farmers increasingly face challenges associated with unpredictable rainfall, water scarcity, land degradation, changing weather patterns, crop losses, limited access to appropriate technologies and inadequate access to agricultural knowledge.

Goreale Agricultural Innovators was established to contribute practical ideas and community-based approaches to these challenges.

We aim to encourage farmers and young people to view agricultural challenges not only as problems, but also as opportunities for innovation, experimentation, learning and sustainable development.

What We Believe

We believe sustainable agricultural development begins with communities. Solutions are more effective when farmers, young people, local leaders, technical experts, schools, organizations and other stakeholders participate in identifying challenges and developing practical responses.

We also believe that innovation does not always require expensive technology. Simple, affordable and locally appropriate solutions can make a significant difference when they are properly understood, tested, adapted and adopted by communities.

Our approach therefore combines local knowledge with modern agricultural practices, technology, research, experimentation and community participation.

Our Focus Areas

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Promoting agricultural practices that help farmers improve productivity while strengthening resilience to climate variability and environmental challenges.

Water Efficiency

Encouraging responsible and efficient use of water through practical irrigation approaches, water conservation and improved agricultural practices.

Sustainable Farming

Supporting farming methods that protect soil, improve crop management, maintain productivity and contribute to long-term food security.

Environmental Conservation

Supporting tree planting, environmental restoration, natural resource protection and community awareness against environmental degradation.

Youth Participation

Creating opportunities for young people to participate in agriculture, environmental action, innovation, research, volunteering and community development.

Agricultural Innovation

Encouraging practical ideas, pilot projects and technologies that can help farmers address production, water, environmental and market-related challenges.

Our Approach

Our approach is practical, participatory and community-focused. We seek to move beyond discussions by encouraging field activities, demonstrations, learning sessions, pilot projects, research and collaboration.

We work toward creating opportunities where farmers can see, test and learn about practical agricultural technologies and management practices before making decisions about adoption.

We also recognize the importance of knowledge sharing. Farmers possess valuable local experience, while agricultural experts, researchers and young innovators can contribute new ideas and technical perspectives. Bringing these forms of knowledge together can strengthen community solutions.

Community Participation

Goreale Agricultural Innovators encourages active participation from farmers, youth, students, schools, community organizations, local leaders, agricultural professionals, environmental practitioners, researchers, development partners and supporters.

Community members can participate through agricultural demonstrations, climate-smart farming activities, tree planting, environmental restoration, research, training, volunteering, pilot projects and sharing innovative ideas.

Through collective action, we aim to strengthen local capacity and encourage communities to develop solutions that are appropriate to their environmental, agricultural and economic circumstances.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To promote practical agricultural innovation, climate-smart farming, efficient water use, environmental conservation and youth participation to strengthen resilient and sustainable farming communities.

Our Vision

A productive, climate-resilient and environmentally responsible agricultural community where farmers, young people and partners work together to create sustainable livelihoods and protect natural resources.

Our Objectives

  • Promote climate-smart and sustainable agricultural practices.
  • Encourage efficient and responsible use of agricultural water.

  • Encourage youth participation in agriculture and environmental innovation.
  • Promote tree planting, restoration and protection of natural resources.
  • Encourage community action against deforestation and environmental degradation.
  • Support agricultural research, experimentation and demonstration activities.

Looking Ahead

Goreale Agricultural Innovators seeks to grow as a practical platform for agricultural and environmental innovation. As the initiative develops, we aim to establish stronger community partnerships, support more demonstration activities, expand research and learning opportunities, and connect local innovators with organizations and institutions that can help turn ideas into practical solutions.

We see significant opportunities in areas such as efficient irrigation, climate-resilient crops, sustainable soil management, environmental restoration, tree planting, agricultural technology, youth entrepreneurship and community research.

Our long-term goal is to contribute to communities where agriculture remains productive while natural resources are protected for future generations.

Founder and Leadership
