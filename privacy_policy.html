Privacy, Consent & Terms

Goreale Agricultural Innovators is committed to responsible handling of personal information, transparency and respect for the rights of individuals.

Effective Date: 23 August 2026

1. Privacy Policy

1.1 Who We Are

Goreale Agricultural Innovators is an initiative working in agriculture, environmental conservation, water conservation, youth participation and sustainable community development.

For purposes of personal-data processing, Goreale Agricultural Innovators will identify and communicate the appropriate data-controller or data-handler information applicable to the relevant activity.

1.2 Purpose of This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and otherwise process personal information submitted through our website, applications, participation forms, partnership forms, contact channels and related activities.

1.3 Personal Information We May Collect

Depending on the service or form, we may collect:

  • Full name and contact details.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • County, location or organization information.
  • Partnership, volunteer or participation information.
  • Skills, professional background and areas of interest.
  • Messages, enquiries, proposals and other information voluntarily submitted by you.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure and maintain the website.

1.4 Information We Do Not Normally Request

General website forms should not be used to submit sensitive personal information unless the relevant service specifically requires it and appropriate safeguards have been provided.

1.5 Why We Process Personal Information

  • To respond to enquiries and requests.
  • To process partnership and collaboration applications.
  • To process volunteer and Get Involved applications.
  • To coordinate agricultural, environmental, water and community activities.
  • To communicate with applicants, partners, volunteers and stakeholders.
  • To maintain legitimate administrative records.
  • To improve our programs, website and services.
  • To meet applicable legal, security and accountability requirements.

1.6 Lawful Basis for Processing

We will seek to process personal information on an appropriate lawful basis under applicable Kenyan data-protection law. Where consent is relied upon, consent will be requested through a clear affirmative action and for specified purposes.

Consent is not intended to replace another lawful basis where the law permits or requires processing on another basis.

1.7 Data Minimisation

We aim to collect information that is adequate, relevant and reasonably necessary for the specific purpose for which it is requested.

1.8 Accuracy

We expect information provided by users to be accurate and reasonably up to date. Where inaccurate information is identified, reasonable steps may be taken to correct or update it.

1.9 Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Security notice: No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should therefore avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary website forms.

2. Data Sharing & Third Parties

2.1 When Information May Be Shared

Personal information may be shared where reasonably necessary for a legitimate purpose, where required by law, where you have consented, or where another lawful basis permits the disclosure.

2.2 Service Providers

We may use third-party providers for services such as website hosting, database hosting, email delivery, security, analytics, payment processing or other technical services.

Where a third party processes personal information on our behalf, appropriate contractual, technical and organizational safeguards should be considered according to the nature of the processing.

2.3 Donations & Payment Platforms

Donations may be processed through external payment platforms. When you follow a payment link, the external provider may collect and process information under its own privacy policy and terms.

2.4 International Transfers

Some technology or hosting providers may process information outside Kenya. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, appropriate safeguards, legal requirements and applicable data-subject rights will be considered.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

3.1 Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate organizational requirements, dispute resolution, security, accountability or applicable legal obligations.

3.2 Deletion

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, appropriate steps may be taken to securely delete, anonymize or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable retention obligations.

3.3 Application Records

Partnership, volunteer and participation applications may be retained for appropriate administrative, communication, safeguarding and accountability purposes.

4. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information.

Right to Be Informed

To receive information about how personal information is processed.

Right of Access

To request access to personal information held about you, subject to applicable law.

Right to Correction

To request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal information.

Right to Object

To object to certain processing of your personal information where permitted.

Right to Deletion

To request deletion of personal information where the legal requirements are satisfied.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may request withdrawal of that consent.

Right to Restrict Processing

To request restriction of processing where applicable under law.

Right to Data Portability

Where applicable, to receive or request transfer of personal information in a legally supported format.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions, exemptions and verification requirements.

5. Consent Notice

5.1 Meaning of Consent

Where consent is used as the lawful basis for processing, it should represent a clear, voluntary, specific and informed indication of the individual's wishes through a statement or clear affirmative action.

5.2 What You Are Told Before Consent

Where consent is requested, the relevant notice should explain who is processing the information, why it is being processed, the types of information involved, relevant sharing, applicable transfers, the right to withdraw consent and the implications of providing, withholding or withdrawing consent.

5.3 Withdrawal

You may contact us to request withdrawal of consent where consent is the applicable lawful basis. Withdrawal does not automatically make prior lawful processing unlawful and may not affect processing that has another lawful basis.

5.4 No Forced Consent

We will seek to distinguish consent from information that is necessary to provide a requested service. Where consent is optional, refusal should not create an unfair disadvantage.

6. Terms & Conditions

6.1 Acceptance

By accessing this website or submitting information through our forms, you agree to use the website responsibly and in accordance with these terms.

6.2 Lawful Use

You must not use this website for unlawful, fraudulent, abusive, malicious or unauthorized purposes.

6.3 Accuracy of Applications

Information submitted through partnership, collaboration, volunteer or Get Involved forms should be accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge.

6.4 Application Does Not Guarantee Acceptance

Submission of an application does not create an automatic entitlement to employment, volunteering, partnership, funding, collaboration, membership or participation.

6.5 Review of Applications

Goreale Agricultural Innovators may review applications and determine participation or partnership based on program requirements, capacity, suitability and organizational priorities.

6.6 Intellectual Property

Unless otherwise indicated, website branding, logos, text, graphics and original materials belong to or are used by Goreale Agricultural Innovators with appropriate permission.

6.7 External Websites

Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience. Third-party websites operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

6.8 Website Availability

We aim to keep the website available and secure, but continuous availability cannot be guaranteed. Maintenance, technical failures, security incidents or circumstances outside our reasonable control may temporarily affect availability.

6.9 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy, Consent Notice or Terms and Conditions when necessary. The updated version will be published on this page together with an updated effective date where appropriate.

6.10 Governing Law

These terms are intended to operate subject to the applicable laws of Kenya.

7. Children's & Young Persons' Information

Some Goreale programs may involve young people. We will take appropriate care when collecting or using information relating to children or other vulnerable participants.

Where applicable, participation and processing of children's information will be handled in accordance with applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements, safeguarding principles and any required parental or guardian authorization.

Safeguarding: Do not submit a child's personal information through a general form unless the form is specifically designed for that purpose.

8. Personal Data Security Incidents

If we become aware of a personal-data security incident, we will assess it and take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and address the incident.

Where notification is required under applicable Kenyan law or regulatory requirements, the relevant notifications will be handled in accordance with those requirements.
