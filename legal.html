Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect and manage personal information.

1. Introduction

GOREALE Agricultural Innovators ("GOREALE", "we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy and personal information of individuals who interact with our organization and website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, store, protect and share personal information.

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, applicable regulations and other relevant laws.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect:

  • Names and other identification details voluntarily provided.
  • Email addresses and telephone numbers.
  • Organization names and professional positions.
  • Enquiries, feedback and contact form submissions.
  • Collaboration and partnership proposals.
  • Information voluntarily provided in connection with donations or support.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information and server logs, where generated.

We aim to collect only information that is relevant and reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.

3. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected when you:

  • Complete a form on our website.
  • Send us an enquiry or message.
  • Submit a collaboration proposal.
  • Participate in our programmes or activities.
  • Communicate with us through official channels.
  • Visit our website and generate technical server information.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Review collaboration and partnership proposals.
  • Coordinate agricultural and community programmes.
  • Communicate organizational activities and updates.
  • Maintain appropriate organizational records.
  • Improve website functionality and services.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud and unauthorized access.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Where applicable, personal information may be processed on the basis of:

  • Your consent.
  • Taking steps at your request before entering an arrangement.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Protection of vital interests where applicable.
  • Performance of a task in the public interest where legally applicable.
  • Other lawful grounds permitted under applicable legislation.

6. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary and legally permitted.

  • Authorized organizational personnel.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Project partners where sharing is necessary and lawful.
  • Relevant public authorities where required by law.

We seek appropriate safeguards when service providers handle personal information on our behalf.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through public forms.

8. Data Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal and accountability requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize or otherwise dispose of it where appropriate and lawful.

9. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use essential technical features necessary for its operation. Hosting services may also generate technical logs.

If analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, appropriate information and consent will be provided where required by law.

10. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites and third-party services.

We do not control the privacy practices of independent external websites. Users should review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

11. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about the use of your personal information.
  • Access personal information held about you.
  • Object to the processing of your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or misleading information.
  • Request deletion of false or misleading information as provided by law.
  • Exercise other rights available under applicable data protection legislation.

You may also raise a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

Official information: Office of the Data Protection Commissioner

12. Children's Information

We do not knowingly request children's personal information without appropriate parental or guardian involvement and any consent required by law.

Activities involving children or young persons shall be handled with appropriate child protection and data protection safeguards.

13. International Data Transfers

Some hosting or technical service providers may process information outside Kenya. Where personal information is transferred outside Kenya, applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards must be observed.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our operations, website features, data practices or applicable laws.

Updated versions will be published on this page.

15. Privacy Enquiries

For privacy questions, access requests or correction requests, please contact GOREALE Agricultural Innovators through our official communication channels.

Visit our Contact Us page to submit a request.

Please do not submit passwords, financial account credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information through general enquiries.
