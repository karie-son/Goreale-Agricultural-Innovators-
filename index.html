Who We Are

Goreale Agricultural Innovators is a youth-led agricultural innovation initiative based in Goreale Ward, Lagdera Constituency, Garissa County, Kenya. We are committed to advancing agriculture through knowledge, research, innovation, technology and sustainable farming practices.

Our work focuses on climate-smart agriculture, afforestation, agroforestry, sustainable crop production, livestock management, soil and water conservation, agricultural technology and agribusiness development. We promote practical solutions that improve agricultural productivity, strengthen livelihoods and support responsible natural resource management.

We recognise agriculture as a foundation for food security, employment, economic development and community resilience. We therefore value agricultural education, research, skills development and the sharing of practical knowledge to empower farmers and strengthen agricultural communities.

We encourage the adoption of modern farming techniques while respecting indigenous knowledge and locally appropriate solutions. By combining traditional experience with scientific knowledge and emerging technologies, we seek to promote productive and climate-resilient farming systems.

Our commitment also extends to environmental conservation through afforestation, biodiversity protection, improved soil health and responsible water use. We recognise the importance of addressing climate change and protecting natural ecosystems for present and future generations.

Through awareness, knowledge sharing, community participation and collaboration, Goreale Agricultural Innovators aspires to contribute to a future where agriculture is productive, communities are empowered and the environment is sustainably protected.

Our Purpose & Commitment

At Goreale Agricultural Innovators, we believe that sustainable agriculture is essential to building resilient communities, strengthening livelihoods and securing a better future for generations to come.

Our purpose is to encourage agricultural innovation, promote climate-smart farming, strengthen knowledge and practical skills, and inspire meaningful community participation in agricultural development and environmental conservation.

We are committed to promoting responsible farming practices, supporting farmers, encouraging youth and community involvement, protecting natural resources and creating awareness of sustainable agricultural opportunities.

Through collective action, continuous learning and innovative thinking, we seek to contribute to a productive agricultural sector, empowered communities and a healthier environment.
