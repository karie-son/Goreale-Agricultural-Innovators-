RE: APPLICATION FOR THE MEKATILILI MICRO-GRANT – GOREALE AGRICULTURAL INNOVATORS

Dear Mekatilili Micro-Grant Selection Committee,

On behalf of Goreale Agricultural Innovators, I am pleased to formally submit our application for consideration under the Mekatilili Micro-Grant Programme. We are seeking support to advance an existing youth-led agricultural innovation initiative that combines digital technology, community engagement and practical agricultural information to address challenges affecting farming communities.

Goreale Agricultural Innovators is a youth-led initiative focused on making agricultural information more accessible, strengthening communication with farming communities and promoting innovative approaches to sustainable agriculture. The initiative is being represented in this application by Samira Abdirahman Maalim, applying in her individual capacity as the lead innovator.

An important achievement of the initiative is that its online platform has already been developed and implemented. This provides an existing digital foundation upon which the next stage of the innovation can be built. The platform is intended to serve as a formal digital communication and information-sharing channel connecting the initiative with farmers and other relevant community stakeholders.

However, the existence of a digital platform is only the beginning. We recognize that meaningful innovation must be tested in real community settings, informed by user experiences and improved according to actual needs. Our immediate priority is therefore to move from platform development to practical implementation, community onboarding, testing and evaluation.

Through this application, we are requesting KES 100,000 to support a focused three-month implementation pilot. The proposed activities will include:

  • Technical review, activation and improvement of the existing online platform.
  • Introducing the platform to an initial group of farmers and community users.
  • Establishing a structured digital communication channel for agricultural information and updates.
  • Sharing relevant information on irrigation, crop-pest management and climate-smart agriculture.
  • Collecting user feedback and assessing the usefulness of the platform.
  • Documenting implementation results and developing a practical plan for future expansion.

We believe the strength of this proposal lies in its readiness to move beyond the conceptual stage. Rather than beginning with an untested idea, we are building on an existing digital platform and seeking resources to demonstrate its practical value through direct community participation.

We also recognize that the long-term development of Goreale Agricultural Innovators will require considerably more investment than the requested microgrant. Future development will involve strengthening technical infrastructure, expanding field operations and establishing a dedicated physical office to support coordination, stakeholder engagement and the continued growth of the initiative.

Nevertheless, our immediate request is deliberately focused. We intend to use the Mekatilili Micro-Grant to achieve a realistic and measurable implementation milestone within three months. The evidence and lessons generated during this period will guide further development and strengthen our ability to pursue additional partnerships and investment.

Our commitment

We are committed to responsible use of the grant, transparent implementation, community participation, measurable progress and proper documentation of the results achieved during the pilot period.

We would be grateful for the opportunity to have our application considered. Your support would provide an important opportunity to demonstrate how an existing youth-led digital innovation can be translated into practical community value and a foundation for wider agricultural development.

Thank you for your time and consideration. We look forward to the possibility of contributing to the objectives of the Mekatilili Micro-Grant Programme.

Yours faithfully,

Samira Abdirahman Maalim
